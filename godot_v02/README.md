# Runner & God v0.2

Same-device 2-player Android co-op prototype rebuilt in Godot.

## Roles
- 1P Runner: bottom-left arrows + bottom-right JUMP.
- 2P God: top toolbar + main play field.

## God powers
- CREATE: drag to draw a temporary cyan platform.
- MOVE: drag a created platform while the runner rides it.
- FREEZE: tap a slime to stop it for 4 seconds.
- LIFT: tap near the runner for a vertical rescue boost.

The stage contains gaps that the runner cannot clear alone, moving-platform cooperation, enemy timing, energy crystals, a checkpoint, particles, parallax scenery and a finish portal.
